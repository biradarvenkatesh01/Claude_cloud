# Handoff: KAGADA 2026 Photo Booth Design

> **For ChatGPT:** This file sums up an earlier conversation so you can pick it up from here. Read it all, then help the user with the next step. The user writes casually and quickly (sometimes by voice), in a mix of English and Kannada/Hindi slang. Keep replies practical, creative and colourful.

---

## 1. Who the user is and what they're doing

- The user is a student volunteer at **IEEE UVCE** (the IEEE student branch at University Visvesvaraya College of Engineering, K.R. Circle, Bengaluru).
- They are organising **KAGADA 2026**, IEEE UVCE's 22nd annual national-level technical student conference.
  - **Date:** 24 October 2026
  - **Venue:** UVCE, Bengaluru
  - **Website:** https://kagada2026.live (code: GitHub repo `biradarvenkatesh01/kagada_01`)
- **Task:** design a **photo booth** for participants to take pictures in on event day.
- **"Kagada" (ಕಾಗದ) means "paper" in Kannada.** All the ideas so far build on this.

## 2. Constraints, from most to least important

1. **DIY only.** They'll build it themselves from cheap, everyday materials: cardboard boxes, chart paper, crepe paper, paper plates, paint, tape, marigolds, fairy lights and so on. No professional printing or fabrication.
2. **Very low budget** ("we are broke"). Aim for **₹200–800 total**.
3. **About 3 days to build.**
4. **Be creative and colourful.** The user rejected "lame", basic ideas (like plain chart paper with text on it). They want **bright, mixed colours, not only the website's maroon/burgundy**.
5. **Size:** similar to a booth they already have (the "Geoshakthi" booth at UVCE): a freestanding board of **about 6 ft wide × 8 ft tall** on a two-leg metal stand.
   - It has a rectangular window cut in the middle, about 3.2 × 3.2 ft, from roughly 2.9 ft to 6 ft above the floor.
   - Decorative parts break out of the outline; Geoshakthi has satellite solar panels sticking out.
   - They may **reuse this stand and board** and redo the front.

## 3. Brand reference (optional to use)

- **Website palette:** burgundy `#5A182B`, parchment `#D8D3C7`, dark `#431220`, KAGADA red `#C0262D`.
  - The site has an origami / paper-texture style and an origami illustration of the UVCE heritage building.
- **Wordmark:** "kagada 2026" in a decorative Devanagari-style Latin font (Saman).
- **Event tracks:** Paper Presentation, Poster Presentation, Project Presentation, Ottige Kaliyona (a social initiative for government school students) and Food For Cause (charity food stalls).
- **Curtain raiser:** Kagathon, a mini-marathon on 17 Oct.
- **Hashtag:** #KAGADA2026

**Recommended for 3 days and no budget:** #7 Paper Plate Mandala, #6 Kagada TV, #3 Comic Book POW!, and #4 Habba Gate for the most festive option.

**Add-ons:**
- Crepe streamers or fairy lights can go on any booth for about ₹100.
- Make 5–6 prop sticks from leftover chart paper: speech bubbles, stars, a KA-GA-DA! starburst.

**3-day plan:**
- **Day 1:** pick the booth, collect boxes, buy paper and paint, assign teams.
- **Day 2:** build (painting, folding and cutting in parallel).
- **Day 3:** assemble on the spot and take test photos.

**Tips:**
- Face the booth toward the light. A bright window behind it makes faces dark.
- Hang a plain cloth about 1–1.5 m behind the window.
