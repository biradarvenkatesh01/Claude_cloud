Zoho's chatbot options,
and why they fall short for us.

Zoho SalesIQ comes with our CRM Plus plan, so I checked every way it lets us put a chatbot on the website. For each option, the pages ahead show a sample chat: one question it handles well, and one it can't.

06 · A problem with all three options

The chat window can't look like our website

Whichever bot type we pick, the chat window itself is SalesIQ's ready-made template. We can only change what Zoho's settings allow. The new website will have its own fonts, colours and layout, and the chat window would still look like a Zoho add-on sitting on top of it.
