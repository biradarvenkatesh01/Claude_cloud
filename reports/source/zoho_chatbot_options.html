Chatbot review · Zoho SalesIQ

Zoho's chatbot options,
and why they fall short for us.

In the last plan I said we would try Zoho SalesIQ first, since it already comes with our CRM Plus plan. Before setting up a test page, I went through every way SalesIQ lets us put a chatbot on the website. This note explains each one in simple words: what it is, how it works, and where it falls short of what we need.

Prepared for DataSphere Edu SolutionsPrepared by Venkatesh BiradarDate 12 Oct 2026
What we needA bot that answers course questions correctly, suggests the right course, and saves every interested student as a lead in Zoho CRM.
What Zoho offersThree ways to build a bot in SalesIQ: a flow builder (Zobot), a FAQ bot (Answer Bot), and Zobot with SalesIQ Scripts.
The short answerEach one covers part of the job, but none can talk to a student and suggest courses. Zoho CRM stays; the bot should be ours.

How Zoho puts a chatbot on the website

All of Zoho's options work through SalesIQ. We paste one small script on the website and a chat bubble appears in the corner. What changes between the options is the "brain" behind that bubble, meaning how the bot decides what to reply.

StudentOpens the website and clicks the chat bubble

→
Bot brain · pick one1. Zobot (flow builder)
2. Answer Bot (FAQs)

When I checked our SalesIQ account, the Bot settings show Zobot and Answer Bot. Zobot currently says "No bots found", so whichever option we choose starts from zero. There is nothing old to break.

03 · Option 1

Zobot: the no-code flow builder

What it is. A drag-and-drop builder where we draw the conversation as a flowchart: greet, ask the name, ask the phone number, show course buttons, then save the lead.

BotHi! What are you looking for today?
ButtonsCoursesFeesTalk to us
BotGreat. What's your name?  →  Phone number?  →  Which course?
ResultLead saved in Zoho CRM

03 · Option 1 — continued

How it works

  • The student mostly clicks buttons. The bot follows exactly the path we drew, step by step.
  • Each answer can be saved into a Zoho CRM field, so lead capture is built in.
  • It can pass the chat to a team member, or show our number outside office hours.

Where it falls short

NeedResultWhy
Correct answersDoesn't fitIt only knows the text we typed into each step. If a student types a question in their own words, it can't understand it.

Easy to updatePartlyFees and dates live inside the flowchart blocks, so every change means finding and editing the right blocks by hand.
Saves leadsFitsThis is what it does best.

Example. A student types: "I'm in 3rd year ECE and know a bit of Python. What should I do for a data job?" The flow bot can't read this. It can only show its menu buttons again.
04 · Option 2

Answer Bot: replies from our FAQs

What it is. Zoho's ready-made FAQ bot. We add FAQs, articles or web pages to SalesIQ, and when a student asks something, it finds the closest one and replies with it. Newer versions use Zoho's AI (Zia) to reword the answer so it reads naturally.

Student asks"What is the fee for Data Analytics?"
→
SearchLooks through the FAQs and articles we added
→
MatchPicks the closest article
→
ReplyAnswer from that article, reworded by Zia

05 · Option 3

Zobot with SalesIQ Scripts

What it is. The same Zobot, but instead of drawing a flowchart, we write code in Deluge, Zoho's own scripting language. Zoho runs our code every time a student sends a message, and the code decides the reply.

// every rule has to be written by hand
if (message.contains("fee")) {
    reply = "The Data Analytics course fee is ...";
} else if (message.contains("python")) {
    reply = "You could start with ...";
} else {
    reply = "Sorry, I didn't get that.";
}

A simplified sketch of how a script bot decides replies. It only does what the rules say.

What SalesIQ still does well

  • It's included in our CRM Plus plan, and its leads land straight in Zoho CRM.
  • It also connects to WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger, where most of our leads come from. Real advantages, but they don't make up for the missing course advice.

What I suggest

Go with Option B from the earlier proposal: a custom chatbot built on our own course content (RAG + an AI model). It reads our course details, talks to the student in normal language, suggests courses, and sends every interested student to Zoho CRM as a lead.

StudentAsks in their own words
→
Our botLooks up our course info, then the AI writes the answer and suggests courses
→
Zoho CRM PlusLead saved as "Website – chatbot"
NeedOur bot (planned)How
Correct answersFitsAnswers only from our course info. If something isn't there, it says so and offers a call-back.
Suggests coursesFitsAsks 1–2 questions about background and goal, then suggests 1–3 courses.
Easy to updateFitsAll course info lives in one sheet. The team edits the sheet; the bot uses the new info.
Saves leadsFitsSends name, phone and course to Zoho CRM through Zoho's API.
Hands overFitsOffers a call-back or our number whenever it can't help.

How we'd get there

Step 1Put all course info in one sheet: fees, dates, syllabus, who it's for
Step 2Build the bot on it and test it with the same 25–30 real questions
Step 3Connect it to Zoho CRM with source "Website – chatbot"
DecisionThe team reviews the test results before it goes live

What stays the same: Zoho CRM is still where every lead lands, so the team's follow-up doesn't change.
